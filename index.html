        About this data

        PanelSourceTagUpdatedFiles

        Live = fetched automatically by a GitHub Action. Manual = entered by hand from a named public document. Linked = an external tool we link to rather than copy. Public sources only; no sell-side research. Panels with no data say "awaiting data" rather than show a placeholder.

        How the labels and checks work

        • What the data says labels are mechanical: a change inside the flat band is "flat". Thresholds are in config/signals.json.
        • A red data issue banner appears when a fetch fails or data goes stale (limits in config/health.json); the page keeps showing the last good data.
        • News keywords, filters and the digest's headline ranking are in config/keywords.json.
        • Quarterly KPIs are typed in from earnings releases and filings in data/manual/company_kpis.json and data/manual/tmdx_kpis.json; every field carries its own source and date.
        • The JOBY share count is in config/holdings.json; OPTN region membership is in config/optn_regions.json.

        Glossary

        DBD
        Donation after brain death. The donor is declared dead by neurological criteria; organs are recovered with circulation maintained.
        DCD
        Donation after circulatory death. Organs are recovered after the heart stops, so timing is tighter and perfusion is used more often.
        NRP
        Normothermic regional perfusion: restoring warm, oxygenated blood flow to the organs in the donor after circulatory death, before recovery.
        OPO
        Organ procurement organization: the nonprofit that manages donation and organ recovery in a defined area.
        OPTN
        Organ Procurement and Transplantation Network: the national U.S. system, overseen by HRSA, that runs the waiting list and publishes these data.
        NM
        Nautical miles (1 NM ≈ 1.15 statute miles), used by OPTN for donor-hospital-to-transplant-center distance.
        Discard rate
        Share of organs recovered for transplant that are not transplanted (OPTN "all organs discard rate").
        Kidney (incl. kidney-pancreas)
        The OPTN weekly dashboard counts combined kidney-pancreas transplants and listings under Kidney. OPTN's annual national reports count them separately, so those panels say "Kidney (alone)".
