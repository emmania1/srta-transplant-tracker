Donor mix: DBD vs. DCD

Live

DBD = donation after brain death. DCD = donation after circulatory death; DCD recoveries are more time-sensitive and lean on perfusion. Mix comparison: OPTN's national reports are annual, so the current year to date is compared with the prior full year as shares, not volumes.
